## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign by posting this comment on the pull
request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the date and the pull request in the repository. You only need to sign once.
If you contribute on behalf of an employer that holds rights to your work, contact us before submitting so the
employer can sign a corporate agreement.
